Green Mask Blockworld

An endless world of desert, red rock, plains, forest, jungle and snowy peaks. Dig, build, make tools, fight monsters, and find portals to two boss dimensions.

PLAY TOGETHER

Tap Play Together, type your name and a server code, and tap Join. Friends who type the same code land in the same world. You see each other, every block anyone digs or builds shows up for everyone, and you can send quick messages like "Follow me!". Your own world stays saved.

BLOCKWORLD 3D

Tap the 3D button and a portal opens under your feet. You fall into a 3D world built from your biomes. Drag to look around, use W A S D to walk, Space to jump, click to dig and right-click to build. Your bag works in both worlds. Tap 2D to go home.

TWO BOSSES

Neptune: make a Neptune Portal from 2 Gold Ingots and 2 Rubies, place it, and walk in to fight the Storm Spot.

The Skylands: deep underground there are ancient stone rooms with a glowing Sky Portal inside. Look for a stone pillar with a cloud on top. The room is far below it. Beat Onyx the Black Dragon and you win wings, so you can fly anywhere.

The Ember Realm: a fire dimension full of lava, Fire Ore and fire monsters. Find the Fire Portal at the bottom of a desert trap temple, or make one from 3 Iron Ingots and 4 Coal. It goes on forever, with four fire biomes: Ember Wastes, Basalt Spires, Glowshroom Forest and Ash Dunes. Ember Fortresses with fire treasure keep showing up. Far to the east, Magmor the Lava Golem waits in his arena. Jump over his fire waves!

The Moon: low gravity, so you jump high and fall slowly. Find a meteor crater in the desert, plains or red rock lands, and there is a Rocket Ship at the bottom. Walk into it for blast off! You can also build a rocket from 2 Fire Gems, 2 Crystal and 2 Gold Ingots. Dig Star Ore with a Fire Pickaxe for Star Shards. Far to the east, the Crater King guards its arena.

STRUCTURES

Trap temples stand in the desert and red rock lands. Watch for spike pits and dart traps. Drop down the hole in the middle for iron tools, armor and the Fire Portal. Villages are in the plains and forest, igloos in the snow, and mineshafts run deep underground. Dig a chest to open it.

ARMOR

Armor takes some of the hit for you. You wear the best armor in your bag automatically. Iron Armor from 6 Iron Ingots, Ruby Armor from 6 Rubies, Fire Armor from 6 Fire Gems. Fire Armor also lets you swim in lava. Star Armor (6 Star Shards) is the strongest. Hold jump while falling to float down slowly.

THE PATH TO GOOD GEAR

Wood Pickaxe from planks, then Stone Pickaxe. Cook Iron Ore with Coal into Iron Ingots for an Iron Pickaxe. Then dig deep for Gold and Ruby.

Monsters come out at night and in dark caves. Hearts come back slowly. When the sky goes dark, Ziggy keeps watch.

Mods & Rules

Mods add new blocks, items, tools, recipes, skins and rules. Rules are little scripts: when something happens, something else happens.

MY MODS

Make your own mod

A mod is a JSON file. Start from the template, change the names and colors, then add it. Blocks can be speckle, ore, brick, bevel, stripes or glow. Items can be a gem, bar or fruit.
